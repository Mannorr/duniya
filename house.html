The House

The whole world.

Dúníyà means “the world”. We are a clothing house in Lagos, designing modern, minimal clothing for women and men and making each piece to order.

Clothing chosen with care.

We design for people who think about what they wear. Our garments are relaxed but deliberate: clear shapes, considered proportions, and details that each have a reason to be there.

You should be able to recognise a DÚNÍYÀ piece by its silhouette, fabric and proportion, not by a logo.

How we work

The house and its garments

01

Intentional design

Every line, pocket and seam is considered. If a detail has no purpose, we leave it out.

02

Cultural expression

Our designs begin in Lagos and draw on where we come from, while staying open to the wider world our name describes.

03

Craftsmanship

Garments are made through our tailoring network, with attention to how each piece is cut, finished and worn.

04

Made to order

We make a garment after you enquire, so we can confirm the fabric, your measurements and the details with you first.

05

Women’s and men’s

Both collections come from the same house and the same approach, each with its own shapes and proportions.

06

Your idea, considered

Besides our own designs, you can ask for a variation or bring us a reference for a custom commission.

Women’s Wear

Same house.
Different expression.

Our women’s designs are their own, not a softer version of the men’s. They are shaped through silhouette, drape, proportion and movement rather than added decoration.
