Questions

Before you order.

How much does a design cost?

Shirts and tops are ₦20,000, trousers ₦20,000, dresses ₦35,000, and any two-piece outfit ₦40,000. The two-piece price applies to any two pieces ordered together.

Are the designs in stock?

Designs are made to order, so your piece is made after we confirm your order. We confirm availability and timing with you.

Do you have standard sizes?

There is no standard size chart yet. Each piece is made from your measurements. If you’re unsure how to measure, choose “Please help me measure” and we’ll take you through it.

Can I change the fabric, colour, length or fit?

Tell us the change in the order form or on WhatsApp. Changes are subject to confirmation. If a change affects the price, we confirm the extra cost with you before production.

How long does it take, and how is it delivered?

Timing, payment and delivery arrangements are confirmed with you before the order goes ahead.

Can you make something that isn’t in the collection?

Choose “custom commission” in the order form or message us on WhatsApp with a reference or your idea. We will tell you honestly whether we can make it and what it will cost.
