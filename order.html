Questions

Before you order.

How much does a design cost?

The price depends on the design and the fabric. Ask about a design on WhatsApp and we will give you a quotation before anything is agreed.

Are the designs in stock?

Designs are made to order, so a garment may not be ready to send straight away. We confirm availability and timing when you enquire.

Can I have a different fabric, colour or length?

Ask us about the change you have in mind. We will tell you whether it is possible for that design and which fabrics are available.

How do measurements work?

We discuss the measurements we need for your piece during your enquiry.

How long does it take, and how is it delivered?

Timing, payment and delivery arrangements are confirmed with you during your enquiry, before the order goes ahead.

Can you make something that isn’t in the collection?

Send us a reference or describe your idea as a custom commission. We will look at it and tell you honestly whether we can make it.
