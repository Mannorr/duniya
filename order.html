Questions

Before you order.

How much does a look cost?

Prices depend on the look and the fabric you choose. Send us the look name on WhatsApp and we will reply with the price.

How long does it take?

Each piece is made after you order. We confirm the lead time with you before cutting begins, so you know when to expect it.

Can I change the fabric or length?

Often, yes. Tell us what you have in mind and we will tell you what works for that garment.

Do you deliver outside Lagos?

Ask us on WhatsApp with your city. We confirm delivery options and cost when you order.

What if it doesn’t fit right?

Tell us. Because every piece is made for you, we will work with you to put it right.
